# GreenMetriX Platform Overview

## What is GreenMetriX
GreenMetriX is an AI-powered sustainability intelligence platform built for Indian manufacturing. It provides real-time monitoring of energy consumption, CO₂ emissions, anomaly detection, and decarbonisation planning across multiple factory sites using live telemetry data, CEA grid emission factors, and ISO 50001 energy standards.

## Core Modules
- **Live Dashboard**: Real-time energy (kWh) and CO₂ (kg) monitoring per factory shift
- **Anomaly Detection**: Isolation Forest ML model flags abnormal energy consumption events
- **Digital Twin**: Physics-informed scenario simulator for what-if energy planning
- **Sustainability Score**: Composite index based on intensity, renewable share, and anomaly rate
- **City Carbon Map**: Geographic overlay of factory emission intensity across Delhi NCR
- **AI Copilot**: Natural language interface for queries on emissions, anomalies, and planning
- **Predictive ML**: Gradient Boosted regression model for 24-hour energy forecasting

## Target Users
Plant energy managers, sustainability officers, and corporate ESG teams at manufacturing facilities in India.

## Data Coverage
8 Delhi NCR manufacturing facilities across sectors: Steel, Auto Components, Chemicals, Textiles, Electronics, Cement.
